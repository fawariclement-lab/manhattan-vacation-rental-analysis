# Manhattan Vacation Rental Investment Analysis

**Tools:** Excel · PivotTables · Data Cleaning · Revenue Modeling

## Overview

A data-driven look at the Manhattan vacation-rental market for an investor deciding **where to buy and what size property to buy**. The analysis covers **2,400+ Manhattan Airbnb listings**, combining listing data with calendar data to connect demand patterns to revenue estimates, and ends in neighborhood- and property-size-specific investment recommendations.

## Approach

- Cleaned and combined listing and calendar datasets in Excel
- Used **PivotTables** to compare demand, supply, and pricing across neighborhoods and property sizes
- Estimated revenue from calendar/occupancy patterns to rank opportunities on earning potential, not just nightly rate

## Key findings

- Identified the **10 highest-demand neighborhoods** in Manhattan for vacation-rental investment
- **1-bedroom properties are the most prevalent** rental type – but the best opportunity is size-by-neighborhood specific: **studios are the preferred size for Midtown**
- A Midtown studio stood out with **$29,940 in 30-day revenue** and an estimated **$359,280 annual revenue opportunity**
- Recommendations are given per neighborhood, pairing each area with the property size its demand pattern supports
